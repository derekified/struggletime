how a minute earns its weight

steer (prompt or edit) → check (run it, failing counts) → follow through (edit again or commit). close the loop and the minute counts fully. leave an agent running on its own and it counts at 0.2×. go silent to debug and it fades out over 5 to 12 minutes instead of vanishing.
